<p><em>MODIS NDVI Drought Risk Forecasting Dataset for Karamoja Region, Uganda (2000-2016)</em></p>
<p>This dataset contains processed MODIS Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) data and derived drought indicators for six districts in the Karamoja region of Uganda. The data was used to trigger alerts in the Drought Risk Forecasting (DRF) program, providing early warning capabilities for drought conditions affecting this semi-arid pastoral region.</p>
